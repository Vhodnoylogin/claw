# CLAW — bodies and morphs

CLAW gives male and female werewolves adjustable body proportions. The separate
CLAW Distribution companion derives their shapes from applied human body morphs,
including ordinary human presets assigned by OBody. No wolf preset is required.

Install Elegant Werewolf Replacer for its textures, the appropriate XP32 skeleton,
and the RaceMenu/SKEE morph mechanism for your game runtime. CLAW provides its own
replacement meshes; Elegant's original body meshes are overridden.

This candidate repairs the male waist/collar morph and the female breast/underarm
surface. Breast growth spreads laterally and vertically with a downward shift.
Both weight filenames remain supplied, with equal endpoints for each sex.

This base contains seven runtime files: the CLAW plugin, four body meshes and two
TRI morph files. It supplies no skeleton, textures, test actors or physics host.
Physics, first-person VR and the Anatomy add-on are outside this candidate.

Offline checks and SE transformation checks do not establish final visual quality.
The owner still needs to inspect the textured result. Residual fur/skin continuity
and limited slimming response remain known quality limitations.
