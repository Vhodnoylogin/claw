[center][size=5][color=#D0B17A][b]Description[/b][/color][/size][/center]
Give Skyrim's werewolves the same freedom to change their shape that OBody brings to human characters. A lean person becomes a leaner beast; an athletic person becomes a stronger-looking beast; a fuller body carries its bulk into werewolf form.

[b]CLAW now connects human body morphs to male and female werewolf bodies.[/b] The human presets you already use supply the shape. CLAW translates supported body traits into the wolf's torso, arms, legs and belly. Weight and available human face and stature parameters also influence the beast's head, ears, tail and claws. Human skin and hair colours can tint its existing materials.

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/77016]OBody NG[/url], that variety can also reach werewolves first encountered in beast form. They receive ordinary human preset values through OBody, which CLAW uses to shape the wolf. No separate pack of wolf body presets is needed.

[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
CLAW's bodies are based on [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/93336]Elegant Werewolf Replacer[/url]. Keep Elegant installed for its textures and use [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/1988]XP32 Maximum Skeleton Special Extended[/url] for the skeleton. CLAW supplies the adjustable werewolf meshes and morphs.

Choose the actual male and female bodies on separate installer pages. Male options: [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/46311]HIMBO[/url] or Vanilla. Female options: [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/198]CBBE[/url], CBBE + [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/30174]3BA[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/31126]BHUNP 3.34 Advanced Ver4[/url], or Vanilla. Replacement bodies need working BodySlide morphs; Vanilla uses actor weight. The BHUNP option also needs its own dependencies and a compatible UNP skin, installed separately. All choices remain available. If several body mods are installed, choose the body whose files actually win your conflicts.

The morph mechanism needs [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/19080]RaceMenu[/url], SKSE and the appropriate Address Library for your game runtime. For automatic human preset variety, install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/77016]OBody NG[/url] with its dependencies and human BodySlide presets. Enable CLAW's optional OBody integration in the installer only if you also want preset inputs for actors first encountered as beasts; human-to-wolf links do not require that extra host. Follow OBody's body-building instructions so the human bodies have working morphs. The CLAW download contains neither human bodies nor preset packs.

[b]Updating a save:[/b] existing characters retain their stored conversion matrices. Start a new game to apply all new defaults from the beginning; reinstalling a body option alone does not reset saved characters.

[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]Technical guide for modders[/b][/color][/size]
Conversion presets, resource paths, compatibility and current limits.
[spoiler]
[b]Version 0.8.0[/b]
The working installer combines CLAW's body resources, native human morph linkage and optional OBody source integration in one archive. It contains male and female TRI morphs, including separate local muscle and soft-size controls. Test actors, inspection-room lighting, human body assets, textures, skeletons, physics configuration and a first-person VR body are not part of the working mod.

[b]Human bodies and conversion presets[/b]
The canonical conversion data contains six matrices: HIMBO male, CBBE female, CBBE 3BA female, BHUNP 3.34 Advanced Ver4 female, and Vanilla for each sex. Their source names and deformation directions follow the actual human resources. The installer selects one male and one female matrix. Matrices are not randomly distributed; OBody distributes ordinary human presets.

The installer recommends from active HIMBO.esp, CBBE.esp, 3BBB.esp and BHUNP3BBB.esp markers. Female recommendation priority is BHUNP, then 3BA, then CBBE, otherwise Vanilla; male priority is HIMBO, otherwise Vanilla. Plugin presence cannot identify the winning meshes when several bodies are installed. The visible selection declares the effective body family.

A wolf preset consists of coefficient and function matrices: multiple human inputs may contribute to one wolf morph, and one input may affect several wolf morphs. Supported functions are linear, positive, negative, smoothstep, integer power and constant. An all-constant custom matrix defines a wolf independently of human values. No executable expressions are evaluated. The game and the matching Morphbench plugin compile the same C++ evaluator. Arbitrary supported source/target names are data; adding a morph does not require rebuilding the evaluator. Missing edges are absent, missing functions are linear, and coefficients with absolute value at most 1e-6 are omitted. Every matrix declares the normalized actor-weight input explicitly.

Existing saved actor matrices remain authoritative after an update. Replacing XML does not silently replace an actor's saved/custom conversion. Mod integrations can explicitly adopt a new profile with ConfigureMorphFamily. See the [url=https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/claw/blob/main/src/main/docs/matrix-contract.md]matrix contract[/url] and matching source on GitHub.

[b]OBody and transformation[/b]
The normal link reads actual human body morph values. It preserves the human source values when the actor transforms and returns. The optional originally-beast integration asks stock OBody to supply an ordinary human preset for an eligible wolf without an observed human source. OBody retains its own assignment, blacklist and distribution rules; CLAW does not promise to override those rules or reroll every beast.

[b]Compatibility and checks[/b]
Install the current [url=https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist?view=msvc-170]Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)[/url] for the native DLL. The [url=https://aka.ms/vc14/vc_redist.x64.exe]official x64 installer[/url] supplies this runtime; its DLLs are not bundled with CLAW.

The archive contains one CommonLib-based DLL for the declared SE, AE and VR runtime boundaries. Technical 0.8 game checks on Skyrim SE 1.7.104.0 / SKSE 2.3.1 with official RaceMenu and OBody NG cover transformation, refresh, six-reference save/load and human return for Vanilla and BHUNP/HIMBO. Vanilla completed with a normal exit. BHUNP/HIMBO passed every mechanical phase and its real 47-key source proof, then ended abnormally during teardown with 0xC0000005; its overall run remains failed, and the cause is not established. The [url=https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/claw/blob/main/src/main/docs/verification-20261102.md]verification record[/url] preserves that limit and distinguishes these checks from earlier HIMBO/3BA and born-werewolf evidence. Offline checks and tests on one runtime are not equivalent to running every runtime or body family. Use the matching SKSE, Address Library and RaceMenu/SKEE resources for your runtime. See the [url=https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/claw/blob/main/src/main/docs/universal-runtime.md]runtime contract[/url].

For the unverified VR route, use SKSEVR, RaceMenu VR and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/58101]VR Address Library for SKSEVR[/url]. The retained base ESP is light-flagged, so its VR loading also needs [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/106712]Skyrim VR ESL Support[/url] and that mod's requirements. This dependency guidance does not establish VR game acceptance.

[b]Resources and current limits[/b]
Werewolf assets are under Meshes/Actors/WerewolfBeast/Character Assets/: malebodywerewolf_0.nif, malebodywerewolf_1.nif, femalebodywerewolf_0.nif, femalebodywerewolf_1.nif, malebodywerewolf.tri and femalebodywerewolf.tri. Let CLAW's matching files win over Elegant. CLAW.esp supplies the male/female world-model mapping. Custom ArmorAddon overrides must preserve compatible model paths.

Each sex currently has identical neutral _0 and _1 geometry. Shape variety comes from morphs and the explicit weight input. Available FaceGen values and stature participate in head, ear, tail and claw conversion alongside body inputs. An actor first encountered as a wolf has no fabricated human face or colour history. Extreme combinations, female breast naturalness, self-intersections and final artistic calibration remain areas for refinement.

[b]Direct morph example[/b]
Use your own stable contribution key and a loaded Actor reference:
[code]NiOverride.SetBodyMorph(target, "CLAWHeadSize", "MyMod.CLAW", coefficient)
NiOverride.UpdateModelWeight(target)

; Reset only your own contribution:
NiOverride.SetBodyMorph(target, "CLAWHeadSize", "MyMod.CLAW", 0.0)
NiOverride.UpdateModelWeight(target)[/code]
Other mods can contribute additional keys, so one key's value is not the complete visible shape. Never clear another mod's morph keys. The GitHub source and READMEs provide the full API and rebuild information.
