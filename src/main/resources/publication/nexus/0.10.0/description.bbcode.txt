[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]CLAW — Customizable Lycanthrope Appearance and Weight[/b][/color][/size]
<br />
<br />CLAW links a character’s human appearance to their werewolf form. Human body shape influences the werewolf’s body fat, musculature and proportions. The mod works with the player and NPCs, including werewolves that originally spawned in beast form.
<br />[center][img width=600,height=100]https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/1704/images/194796/194796-1791664990-433961669.png[/img][/center]
<br />[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]What changes[/b][/color][/size]
<br />[list]
<br />[*]Body, arm, leg, belly and chest proportions.[/*]
<br />[*]Head and muzzle shape, based on available human traits.[/*]
<br />[*]Ear, tail and claw size, influenced by human stature and proportions.[/*]
<br />[*]Optional physics for ears and tails, plus female breast motion.[/*]
<br />[*]Coat appearance, using ten variants from Elegant.[/*]
<br />[/list]Human hair colour determines the colour group, while a stable individual seed selects a variant within that group. Werewolves without a known human appearance receive an individual coat selection. The selected coat persists through transformations, model updates and saving/loading.
<br />
<br />[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]Optional physics[/b][/color][/size]
<br />Choose [b]Faster HDT-SMP[/b] in the installer to add motion to werewolf ears and tails, and to female breasts. Install the selected physics mod separately. Choose [b]None[/b] to use CLAW without added physics.

<br />[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
<br />[list=1]
<br />[*]Install the required dependencies.[/*]
<br />[*]For Elegant, download [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/93336?tab=files][b]Elegant Werewolf Player Only FOMOD 4K[/b] or [b]Elegant Werewolf Player Only FOMOD 2K[/b][/url]. Select [b]Randomized[/b] during installation to give werewolves different coats. Other choices provide a single coat.[/*]
<br />[*]Install a supported replacement body to transfer its human morphs to the werewolf form. For HIMBO, CBBE, 3BA and BHUNP, build the body in BodySlide with [b]Build Morphs[/b] enabled. With vanilla bodies, werewolf body shape uses the character’s current weight.[/*]
<br />[*]Install CLAW through its FOMOD. Select your actual male and female human bodies and choose a physics option. Let CLAW win file conflicts with Elegant.[/*]
<br />[*]Install OBody if you want varied human presets. These differences will also be reflected in werewolf forms. The installer option [b]“Use OBody presets for wild werewolves”[/b] assigns source presets to eligible characters that originally spawned as werewolves. Without this option, CLAW uses their existing available parameters.[/*]
