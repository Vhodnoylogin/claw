[size=4][color=#D0B17A][b]Individual coats[/b][/color][/size]
CLAW gives each werewolf an individual coat drawn from 34 patterns from [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/93336]Elegant Werewolf Replacer[/url]. Human hair colour determines a suitable colour group, while a stable character seed selects a pattern within that group. Werewolves without an available human hair colour receive a consistent selection from the natural coat pool.

Automatic selection uses 32 natural patterns. Two additional stylised patterns are available through explicit assignments by integrations. CLAW uses Elegant's original texture colours and applies matching textures to the head, body and fur.

Coat choices persist through transformations, model refresh and save/load. Manual assignments take priority over automatic selection; clearing an assignment restores automatic selection. The texture library is included in the CLAW archive, so no separate coat pack or manual extraction is needed. Elegant remains a requirement and is credited as the source of these assets.
