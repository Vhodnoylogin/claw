Added 34 coat patterns from Elegant and individual coat selection for each werewolf. Human hair colour determines the colour group, while a stable character seed selects the pattern within that group. When no human hair colour is available, selection uses the natural coat pool.
Automatic choices and manual assignments are preserved through model refresh and save/load.
Added support for classic UNP Renewal 4.6 using actor weight and available human traits, without BodySlide morphs.
Fixed how human character scale is taken into account when converting ear, tail and claw proportions.
Fixed preservation of individual coat materials when model transparency changes.
Improved preservation of human source parameters; processing does not activate after an unsuccessful save load.
Added body-mod links and an explanation of the OBody option to the installer.
